# Docs sentences

Lead with what the reader can do or will see, then explain how.

- Keep one idea per sentence. Split sentences that need a semicolon.
- Do not use slogans or aphorisms such as "the finger is the clock" in docs. Home-page marketing copy may be shorter but must remain literal.
- Name concrete things: "the header title", not "a piece of chrome".
- Show the result before the rule: put a demo or code sample before a table of cases.
- Use tables for comparisons the reader already has context for. Do not open a page or section with a table.
