Spend a flight's head inside its animation rather than in front of it. A head waited out as a delay leaves the animation uncommitted until the instant it must move, so a first frame that arrives late arrives partway through the curve and the opening is never drawn: painted frames off a phone at 60fps put the first frame a box appeared on at 67% of its travel. Baked as a flat stop the same seconds hold the from-pose while the animation is already running, and a late frame lands inside them. The ghost, the paint channels, the type staircases and the camera all ride the same lead-in, so the flight stays one composition.
