Give the release to whoever owns the screens. A transition that names `current` or `prev` now has flemo decide whether the gesture committed, settle the clock every participant lands on, and hand its `onEnd` that answer as `triggered`, so a hook written only to land an element of its own no longer has to restate the commit rule. A transition that takes the screens over still decides, as before.
