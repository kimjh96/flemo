Put the container transform on the playground bench. Picking `zoom` flies the artwork as a `zoom` morph, whose camera carries the list screen the artwork is small on, so the grid is pushed past the edges instead of sitting still while a card escapes it. It arrives with `aperture`, a transition authored for it: the camera supersedes a moving screen's transform, so `aperture` animates none and spends its length on the fade instead. The bench selects whole cases rather than crossing two axes, because the library states which pairings compose and a free cross product would offer the ones that do not.
