Thin a type morph's leading staircase so no two steps land in the same frame. A fast-opening ease packed several one-pixel steps into the opening frames, and each step is a moment the line-height (a paint) and the lift (a transform) can present a frame apart, which the eye reads as a shimmer. Merging sub-frame-spaced boundaries keeps the staircase and its end boundary while cutting the shimmer.
