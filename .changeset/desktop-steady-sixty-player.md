Route desktop Blink to the rAF player on verified steady-60Hz HiDPI displays. The first flights of a session keep the compiled tier while an in-flight cadence probe measures the panel with a compositor animation live (the only moment an adaptive 120Hz panel shows its true rate); two verified ~60Hz flights graduate the session to the player, which replaces the compiled tier's HiDPI convergence artifacts with velocity-gated device-pixel snapping: fast spans snap crisp, slow tails glide fractionally (device-judged — always-snap ratchets the pop parallax), and the landing governor closes the final sub-pixel approach. A single high-refresh reading latches the session back to compiled permanently, and the render-settle gate turns on for graduated sessions so the main-thread player is never born into the entering screen's mount commit. The gate's give-up path now also rides two consecutive fast frames before releasing, so a pop's returning screen — whose unfreeze re-uses its DOM and never trips the mount-commit detector — has its style/layout block absorbed into the hold instead of stuttering the flight's opening. Desktop behavior at 1x density, on high-refresh panels, and on touch devices is unchanged.
