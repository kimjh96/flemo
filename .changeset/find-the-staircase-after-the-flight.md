Search a type morph's staircase after the flight that needs it rather than during it. A browser instantiates a face at every size it has never rendered, and the search asks for about a hundred new sizes, so the bill landed on the frame a tap started: a session's first push held a frame for 50ms against 16.8ms for every push after it. The first flight of a face now runs on the straight interpolation and the search happens at the next idle moment, so every flight after it is corrected and free.
