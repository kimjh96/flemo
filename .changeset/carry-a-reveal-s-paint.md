Reveal a morph that paints a gradient or a shadow, instead of laying its box out at every size. The image is drawn to the size the transition is at through an animated background-size, the shadow is cast by an empty box travelling under the moving element wearing the card's own box-shadow, and a box whose size used to be painted on whole device pixels now moves as smoothly as its position does.
