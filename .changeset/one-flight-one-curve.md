Run a nested morph on the curve of the flight that carries it. A nested end is not travelling to a place on a moving screen, it is moving inside a box whose size its carrier is interpolating, so a curve of its own makes the box collapse on one shape while the thing inside walks on another. Measured on a paired card and title, the title's baseline stood 30px below the bottom of its own card for about 250ms of every flight.
