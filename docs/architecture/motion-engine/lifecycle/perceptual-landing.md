# Perceptual cut and early landing

`perceptualSpan.ts` permits resolution only after every animated channel of every participant—active, passive, parts, and decorator—has permanently entered its imperceptibility band: less than one device pixel or one alpha step remains. Any unanalyzable participant vetoes the cut.

On the compiled path, the wall-clock cut timer starts at release. Cancel-resume, watchdog, or stall-shift recovery disarms it. Governed touch-WebKit tiers never arm it: presentation does not reliably follow wall time, so a cut could visibly snap.

Early landing releases the arrival hold when every participant is within one CSS pixel or alpha step, using a DPR-1 band at or before the cut. This places reveal layout and paint beneath the subpixel tail. Early landing follows the cut's disarm rules.
